ביס & סיר: three looks

Each of these is the same recipe page, shown at phone width, in Hebrew and right to left. It shows six servings scaled from a base of four, and the cumin amount has been edited. Tap + and − to try the scaling. "Dark" switches all three mockups to their dark token set.

A · Annotated Card Recommended

Calm, personal, editorial

The recipe is a white index card. A serif title sits over a clean sans body, in herb-green ink with one tomato-red button. Edited amounts carry a pencil note in the margin, the way you'd write on a printed recipe.

  • The original-value hint becomes the signature of the app
  • Easy to build with shadcn; mostly colour, font and radius changes
  • Risk: quieter at first glance

B · The Board

Bold, playful, memorable

The cutting board is the motif. Ingredients sit on a solid oak-coloured slab with a handle slot, the type is heavy and large, and the buttons are pill-shaped.

  • The most recognisable of the three, and it ties to the name
  • Risk: the motif can feel gimmicky across many screens (forms, login)
  • More custom styling on top of shadcn

C · Kitchen Ledger

Quiet, precise, numbers first

Black and white with one blue that marks scaled amounts. One sans typeface, thin lines between rows and a numbers column aligned at the start. Very easy to read at arm's length while cooking.

  • The fastest to build; closest to stock shadcn
  • Strong contrast, simple dark mode
  • Risk: looks like many other apps, with little personality for a portfolio
